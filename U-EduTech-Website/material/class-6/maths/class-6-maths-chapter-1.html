📖 Chapter Notes

Important concepts from Patterns in Mathematics.

What is a Pattern?

A pattern is a repeated or regularly changing arrangement of numbers, shapes, objects or ideas. Patterns help us identify relationships and make predictions.

Number Patterns

Number patterns are sequences of numbers that follow a particular rule.

  • 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ... — add 2 each time
  • 5, 10, 15, 20, ... — add 5 each time
  • 10, 20, 30, 40, ... — multiples of 10

Growing Patterns

In a growing pattern, the number or size of objects increases according to a rule.

Repeating Patterns

A repeating pattern repeats the same sequence again and again.

Finding the Rule

To understand a number pattern, compare consecutive terms and identify what operation is being performed. The rule can involve addition, subtraction, multiplication or another mathematical relationship.

Example 3
