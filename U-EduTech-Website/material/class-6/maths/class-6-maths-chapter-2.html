Chapter 2: Lines and Angles

Class 6 Mathematics – Ganita Prakash
Learn about Lines, Line Segments, Rays and different types of Angles.

📘 Chapter Introduction

In this chapter, we learn about basic geometrical ideas such as lines, line segments, rays and angles. These concepts are used in many areas of mathematics and everyday life.

Geometry: Geometry is the branch of mathematics that deals with shapes, sizes, positions and properties of objects.

📏 1. Lines

A line is a straight path that extends endlessly in both directions.

Line: A line has no fixed starting point or ending point.

Example:
←──────────────→

Line Segment

A line segment is a part of a line having two fixed endpoints.

Example:

A────────────B

Here A and B are the endpoints.

Ray

A ray has one fixed endpoint and extends endlessly in one direction.

Example:

A──────────────→

A is the endpoint.

📐 2. Angles

An angle is formed when two rays have a common endpoint.

Vertex: The common endpoint of the two rays forming an angle is called the vertex.
Example:

An angle can be represented as ∠ABC.

Here B is the vertex.

📐 3. Types of Angles

∠

Acute Angle

Greater than 0° and less than 90°.

Example: 45°
∟

Right Angle

Exactly 90°.

Example: 90°
◢

Obtuse Angle

Greater than 90° and less than 180°.

Example: 120°
↔

Straight Angle

Exactly 180°.

Example: 180°
⟳

Reflex Angle

Greater than 180° and less than 360°.

Example: 270°
○

Complete Angle

Exactly 360°.

Example: 360°

⭐ Important Facts

✔ Acute Angle: 0° < Angle < 90°
✔ Right Angle = 90°
✔ Obtuse Angle: 90° < Angle < 180°
✔ Straight Angle = 180°
✔ Reflex Angle: 180° < Angle < 360°
✔ Complete Angle = 360°

🌍 Angles in Real Life

We can see different types of angles around us.

Examples:

🕐 Clock hands form different angles.
🚪 An open door forms an angle.
📖 The corners of a book show right angles.
✂️ Scissors form an angle when opened.

📝 MCQ Practice

1. A line extends:

  • A) In one direction
  • B) In two directions
  • C) Nowhere
  • D) Only upward

2. A line segment has:

  • A) No endpoint
  • B) One endpoint
  • C) Two endpoints
  • D) Three endpoints

3. A right angle measures:

  • A) 45°
  • B) 90°
  • C) 180°
  • D) 360°

4. An angle greater than 90° but less than 180° is:

  • A) Acute angle
  • B) Right angle
  • C) Obtuse angle
  • D) Complete angle

5. An angle of 180° is called:

  • A) Acute angle
  • B) Right angle
  • C) Straight angle
  • D) Reflex angle

✅ Answer Key

1. B) In two directions

2. C) Two endpoints

3. B) 90°

4. C) Obtuse angle

5. C) Straight angle
